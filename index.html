Purkin

Human, by choice.

We want times and places where people are only with each other, and for anyone to be able to find one.

Read ↓

A kind message arrives and you can’t tell who wrote it. A voice on the phone is warm and you aren’t sure it is anyone.

Nobody did anything wrong. It is just getting harder to know when a person is there.

We want it to stay easy to be somewhere a person certainly is.

What we hold

  1. Worth is not a ranking.

    A person does not have to beat a machine to matter. Some things are worth doing because you are the one doing them: learning the song, cooking the meal, keeping the friend. They stay worth doing when a machine does them better, and that includes being better company.

  2. Some things are the doing.

    A meal cooked for you. A letter written to you. An hour someone gives you. What you receive is that a person chose to. Hand it off and it becomes a different thing, however good.

  3. Tools are welcome. Stand-ins are not.

    An oven, a book, a guitar and a hearing aid extend a person. What we leave outside is whatever speaks, listens or decides in a person’s place.

  4. A choice needs somewhere to happen.

    When every screen, earpiece and counter has an assistant in it, the unassisted version stops being the default. It survives only where someone sets it up on purpose.

  5. Everyone is free to come and go.

    Nobody is asked to stop using anything. The people who build AI are as welcome at the table as anyone.

Begin tonight

Hold a Purkin evening.

You don’t need permission, a membership or a sticker. You need a table.

  1. Invite people. Two is enough. If someone can’t travel, a plain call between two people counts.
  2. Put the devices in a bowl by the door. Switched off. No assistants, earbuds or smart glasses. Whatever keeps someone well or on call stays with them.
  3. Say it out loud: “Tonight it’s just us.” Then cook, play, read aloud, mend, make music, argue kindly.
  4. Keep the day. The same evening every week or every month, so people know when to come.
